# First run: "Choose your starter"

Goal: a person with nothing to photograph still gets a catch, a Dex, progress and a reason to open the camera,
in about a minute, with no credit spent, no photo sent and no permission asked before they have seen value.

## Principles
- No dead ends: every step works offline, can be skipped, and ends in the app.
- Teach by doing: see it, pick one, catch it, find where it lives, then go find a second.
- Honest: the starter is labelled a gift; real catches come from the camera and are rated by real rarity.
- Immersive: the app's own realm illustrations as a slow-drifting backdrop, dark in both appearances, haptics on
  every beat, Reduce Motion falls back to crossfades, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type everywhere.
- Adaptive: one column on phones, text beside visual on wide windows (iPhone Duo inner display, iPad-class sizes).

## Flow (4 steps, 5 taps)
1. **Welcome** - three rarity-framed species cards float in a fan with tilt parallax. "Living Dex - Catch the real
   world." Begin.
2. **Choose your starter** - a paging carousel of four real species (Robin, Red Fox, Fly Agaric, Wood Anemone).
   The centred card is the choice; the button names it. Confirming runs the viewfinder: brackets close on the photo,
   shutter flash, haptic, then the real reveal card with NEW DEX ENTRY, XP and streak, captioned "Your starter - a
   gift to begin your Dex". It is saved as a normal catch (known species, no cloud call, no credit; rarity is
   grounded by the Worker in the background and heals later if offline).
3. **Nearby** - "See what lives near you": a preview grid of locked slots. The button asks for location in context;
   on grant it fetches the real regional Dex and counts up "N species live near you - you have 1". Denied is fine:
   a friendly line and Continue.
4. **Ready** - "Now find your second": the three ways in (camera, a photo, a sample), the 30 free identifications
   and the consent promise. Open the camera.

Skip (top right) leaves at any time. After onboarding the Field shows a one-time hint over the photo button for the
person with nothing in reach.

## Not doing
No account, no sign-in wall, no paywall, no notification prompt, no tutorial overlays on the real screens.
